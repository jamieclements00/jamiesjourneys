About

A young history buff
on the road

Jamie's Journeys is a travel blog with a geopolitical bent — equal parts travelogue and history lesson. The premise is simple: the places worth going to are usually the places with something to say, and the best way to understand the world is to move through it.

Based in London, I've spent the last few years working my way across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and now the former Soviet world — with a particular obsession for frontier territories, contested histories, and the places that don't make it onto the standard itinerary. Currently on an extended trip through the Caucasus and Eastern Europe.

The blog is written in something close to real time — notes taken on the ground, posted shortly after. No sponsorships, no itinerary guides, no listicles. Just the places, the history, and whatever I happened to think about while I was there.
